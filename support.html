Cancel a subscription

On iPhone: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Linen → Cancel Subscription. You can also open it from Linen: Me → Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next charge; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

Free trial

The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Linen reminds you one day before the trial ends. If you cancel before then, you are not charged.

Restore a purchase

Bought Premium or Lifetime and reinstalled? Open Me → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Highlights and notes

Your highlights, bookmarks and progress are stored on your phone. Use Me → Export to keep a copy, or turn on backup to keep them across devices.
