A quiet place for God's Word.

Linen is a quiet Bible app. Swipe through Scripture one verse at a time, start and end each day with a verse and a prayer, and find words for how your heart feels today.

  • Read the whole Bible free, one verse per screen. No ads, no card required.
  • A morning and evening verse with a prayer written for you.
  • Verses for when you feel anxious, weary, lonely, afraid, grieving, grateful or joyful.
  • Four translations offline: WEB, BSB, KJV and Douay-Rheims.
  • Highlights, bookmarks, reading progress and dark mode.
