Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Linen is built to be a quiet place. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, reading progress, settings and reminder times are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm which purchases are active on your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Backups

Linen has no accounts and no backup server. Your highlights, saved verses and settings are included in your phone's own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple handles under its privacy policy. You can also export your highlights and saved verses as a text file from Me → Export.

Audio

All audio is built into the app. Playing it does not contact any server.

Notifications

Reminders are off by default and scheduled on your device. Turning them on does not send any information to us.

Children

Linen is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete any data we hold about you by emailing linen@decky.cc. Residents of California, the EU and the UK have these rights under the CCPA and GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
