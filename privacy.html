Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Linen is built to be a quiet place. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

Your highlights, bookmarks, reading progress, settings and reminder times are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm which purchases are active on your device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history for this app. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Optional backup

If you choose to turn on backup with Sign in with Apple or Google, your highlights, bookmarks and progress are stored so you can restore them on a new phone. We use your account only to identify your backup. You can delete your backup and account at any time from the app or by emailing us.

Audio

When you play audio, Linen downloads audio files from our content server. The server keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for up to 30 days for security, and does not link them to you.

Notifications

Reminders are off by default and scheduled on your device. Turning them on does not send any information to us.

Children

Linen is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete any data we hold about you by emailing linen@decky.cc. Residents of California, the EU and the UK have these rights under the CCPA and GDPR.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
